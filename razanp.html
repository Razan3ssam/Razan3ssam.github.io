Consumer Complaint Classification

Financial companies receive thousands of complaints a day. This NLP model reads one and predicts which team should handle it (credit card, credit reporting, debt collection, mortgages and loans, retail banking), with a confidence score.

I cleaned about 162K CFPB complaints, removed 38K duplicates before splitting so test results aren’t inflated, and compared SimpleRNN, LSTM, GRU and a fine-tuned DistilBERT. DistilBERT won with a macro F1 of 0.843, but GRU came close (0.826) and trained about 50 times faster. When the model is unsure, the Gradio app recommends manual review instead of guessing.

Macro F1: SimpleRNN 0.773 · LSTM 0.812 · GRU 0.826 · DistilBERT 0.843

Road Damage Detection

Detects and locates road damage in photos across 7 classes, from potholes to alligator cracking. I trained YOLOv8n and several YOLO11n setups under the same conditions and picked YOLO11n. A Streamlit app lets you upload an image, adjust the threshold and see boxes with confidence scores.

mAP50 59.0% · Precision 63.6% · 61.6 FPS
